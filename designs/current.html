open questions

  • The corner accent is a thin 2px right-edge line at 26%–74% of card height. Lights up on card hover, goes warm gold + glow on direct corner hover. Alternatives in flight: bottom-edge line, three small dots, diagonal stripes, no visible handle.
  • Card blur is currently 4px (slider goes 0–12 in the dev panel). Above ~8 the cards become translucent enough that the rays/shimmer behind start showing through prominently.
  • Background opacity steps surface=0.40, middle=0.24, floor=0.10. Live app interpolates continuously with depth; this static doc shows the three banded states.
  • The water gradient was just darkened — at-rest zone now drops into #00040C at the very bottom for a heavy "settled deep water" feel.
