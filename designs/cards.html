Known weak spots worth iterating on:

  • Middle-zone card readability — currently feels washed out.
  • Floor zone cards can read as "broken" rather than "at rest" — want that calm quality.
  • The ✓ button always feels off; could be a hover-reveal pattern or entirely different.
  • "sinking soon" amber border is subtle but might be too aggressive next to a calm surface card.
  • Typography scale — 13px / 10px is tight on retina but tiny on a 4K monitor.
